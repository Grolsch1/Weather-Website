About Weather Window

Weather Window explores how weather can be communicated through visual experiences rather than numerical information alone.

About the creator

Hi, my name is Christopher Beckenstrater. I love geography and the weather, but I have always had a problem with weather apps and websites. I find that many people, myself included, struggle to comprehend the vast amounts of weather information that these services give you. Therefore, I created Weather Window with the goal of communicating weather to users visually.
